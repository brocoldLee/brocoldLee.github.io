## Abstract

The purpose of this study was to evaluate the effects of aerobic exercise on blood lipid levels in middle-aged obese Korean women through a systematic review and meta-analysis.

Randomized controlled trials published in domestic journals up to July 8, 2024, were identified using RISS, KISS, and DBpia databases. The search keywords included “obesity,” “women,” “aerobic exercise,” and “blood lipids.” A total of 8 studies comprising 16 groups (aerobic exercise and control) and 152 participants (77 exercise, 75 control) were included in the analysis.

Using a random-effects model, significant reductions were observed in total cholesterol (TC; SMD = -1.08; 95% CI: -1.77 to -0.40; I² = 72%; p < 0.001) and LDL-C (SMD = -1.12; 95% CI: -2.25 to 0.01; I² = 88%; p < 0.001). Using a fixed-effects model, a significant increase in HDL-C was observed (SMD = 1.47; 95% CI: 1.09 to 1.84; I² = 39%; p > 0.05), whereas triglycerides (TG) did not show a significant change.

These findings suggest that aerobic exercise is an effective intervention for improving blood lipid profiles in middle-aged obese Korean women, particularly in reducing total cholesterol and LDL-C and increasing HDL-C.
