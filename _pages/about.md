**Welcome to my space!**

I recently received a master’s degree in Exercise Physiology from the University of Seoul, South Korea. During my studies, I explored how exercise influences both physical and psychological outcomes and gained experience conducting research on exercise interventions and behavioral health.

My research interests center on how exercise and physical activity affect human health and performance. I am particularly interested in cognitive and cardiovascular responses to exercise, sports injury, and data-driven approaches to understanding individual differences in these outcomes.



## Beyond Research

Outside of research, I enjoy a wide range of sports, especially soccer. I also enjoy taking on new challenges and gaining new experiences.
