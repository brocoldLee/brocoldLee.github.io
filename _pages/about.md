Welcome to my space. I received a master’s degree in Exercise Physiology from the University of Seoul<br>
pursuing research at the intersection of sports science and health informatics.<br>
My work focuses on applying data-driven approaches to understand human behavior and health outcomes,<br>
with a particular interest in integrating exercise physiology and computational methods.

## Research Interests

- Exercise Physiology  
- Sports Science  
- Artificial Intelligence  
- Public Health  

## Research Vision

My goal is to become a researcher who contributes to improving society through sports science.
