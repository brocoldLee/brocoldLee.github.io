Outside of research, I enjoy staying active, learning new skills, and taking on new challenges. Soccer has been a particularly important part of my life, but I also enjoy exploring different sports, music, and new experiences.

## Sports

Soccer has been a major part of my life, and it remains the sport I enjoy most. I also enjoy weight training, tennis, skiing, and water skiing.

## Music

I enjoy playing the cello and learning music as another way to explore something outside of research and sports.

## New Experiences

I enjoy learning and trying things that are unfamiliar to me. Whether through sports, music, travel, or other activities, I value experiences that allow me to learn something new.
